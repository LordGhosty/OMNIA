# OMNIA branding policy

The OMNIA name, atom logo and OMNIA ASCII wordmark identify the official OMNIA project. This policy distinguishes official releases from independently modified software. It does not claim that a trademark is registered, establish ownership of a mark, or replace a trademark clearance/registration process.

## Software freedoms

OMNIA's source-code license remains GPL-3.0-or-later. You may use, study, modify and redistribute the software under that license. This policy does not add restrictions to those copyright permissions, withdraw existing permissions for artwork, or require removal of copyright/license notices. GPLv3 section 7(e) permits declining trademark rights for product names and service marks.

## Permitted identification and redistribution

- You may identify OMNIA in factual descriptions, reviews, compatibility statements, attribution and links, without suggesting endorsement.
- You may redistribute an unchanged official release, including its original name, artwork and signatures, while satisfying the GPL and third-party license obligations. Passing an unchanged installer to a friend does not make it unofficial or invalidate its signature.
- You may clearly identify an independent project as based on OMNIA. Preserve required notices and clearly disclose modifications.

## Modified products and endorsement

Permission to modify source code is not permission to present a modified product as an official OMNIA release. Independently modified products should use a distinct product name, icon and primary wordmark unless the project owner gives branding permission. Do not claim that a fork is maintained, approved, signed or validated by the official OMNIA project without that authorization. This does not restrict identification, attribution or other uses permitted by applicable law.

The official project's signing credentials are not distributed with the source. Forks must use their own signing identity and explain it to their users.

## Before public release

The project owner must publish the official repository/download locations, the responsible rights-holder/contact and the expected signing identity. These are not yet configured in this development project. The name and artwork should undergo clearance in the intended jurisdictions before registration or public brand claims. Legal enforceability depends on applicable law; this file does not itself prevent impersonation.
